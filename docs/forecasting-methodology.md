## 1. Overview & Statistical Transparency Statement

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Planning Assumption Disclosure:** The 2024 projections in this repository represent an **illustrative scenario-based planning baseline** designed for commercial budgeting, capacity planning, and Power BI visual layout demonstration. 
> 
> They are **not** generated by an automated neural network, ARIMA model, or black-box econometric algorithm. The bounding ranges represent **illustrative planning scenarios** rather than statistically estimated 95% confidence intervals.

## 3. Scenario Calculation Methodology

The scenario range is computed deterministically using standard corporate budgeting multipliers applied to the monthly baseline forecast:

$$\text{Illustrative Lower Scenario (Conservative)} = \text{Baseline Forecast Sales} \times 0.88 \quad (-12\%)$$
$$\text{Illustrative Upper Scenario (Optimistic)} = \text{Baseline Forecast Sales} \times 1.12 \quad (+12\%)$$

### Source Column Mapping Note
In the source dataset `data/raw/fact_forecast_2024.csv`, columns are labeled with legacy headers for backwards schema compatibility:
- `Sales_Lower_95_USD` represents the **Illustrative Lower Scenario Bound** ($0.88 \times \text{Sales}$).
- `Sales_Upper_95_USD` represents the **Illustrative Upper Scenario Bound** ($1.12 \times \text{Sales}$).
- `Orders_Lower_95` and `Orders_Upper_95` represent the corresponding conservative (-12%) and optimistic (+12%) order volume scenarios ($0.88 \times \text{Orders}$ and $1.12 \times \text{Orders}$).

These column headers are retained to maintain structural consistency with DAX measures and Power Query M scripts, while their semantic interpretation is strictly defined as illustrative managerial planning bounds.

## 4. Key Planning Assumptions & Business Drivers

1. **Top-Line Growth:** Full-year 2024 baseline revenue is projected at **$14.84M**, representing an anticipated **+15.6%** expansion over the 2023 actual baseline ($12.84M).
2. **Seasonal Distribution:** Models realistic retail seasonality with Q1 starting at $1.05M/mo and accelerating into Q4 holiday peaks (November–December combined: $2.82M).
3. **Margin Expansion:** Incorporates operational efficiency and procurement scale, projecting gross margin expansion from 18.10% in January to 18.90% in December.
4. **Planning Scenarios:** The ±12% bounding range provides executive leadership with conservative downside coverage ($13.06M) and optimistic upside potential ($16.62M) for inventory and working capital allocation.
