## Key Planning Assumptions

1. **Top-Line Growth:** Projects full-year 2024 revenue of **$14.84M**, representing a **+15.6%** increase over the 2023 baseline ($12.84M).
2. **Seasonal Variation:** Reflects natural retail seasonality with accelerating volume into Q4 (November-December peak holiday surge).
3. **Margin Expansion:** Incorporates operational efficiency gains, expanding profit margins from 18.0% in Q1 to 20.1% in December.
4. **Confidence Intervals:** 95% confidence bands (±10% variance) provide executive leadership with optimistic and conservative boundary planning.
